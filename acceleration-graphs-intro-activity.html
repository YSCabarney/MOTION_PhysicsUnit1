2

The Formula

⏱ 2 min
a = (vf − vo) / t
a = acceleration (m/s²) · vf = final velocity · vo = original velocity · t = time (s)

A: A striker starts from rest and reaches 8.0 m/s in 2.5 s. What is her acceleration?

m/s²

B: A ball rolls at 12 m/s, then slows to 0 m/s over 4.0 s. What is its acceleration? (Watch the sign.)

m/s²

Read the Graphs

⏱ 4 min

Pick an answer. Whether you're right or wrong, you'll see the work that proves it.

1. An object has a constant, positive acceleration and starts from rest. What shape is its position-time graph?

2. A velocity-time graph is a straight line sloping downward. What does that tell you about acceleration?

3. On a v-t graph a soccer ball is at 2 m/s at t = 0 s and 10 m/s at t = 4 s. What is its acceleration?

4. A player runs at a steady 5 m/s down the wing. What does her acceleration-time graph look like?
